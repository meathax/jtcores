Sound CPU address decoding, E7 (PAL16L8)
Decoded from the fusemap dumped by Caius

Inputs:

1  - /M1
2  - /RD
3  - /RFSH
4  - /MREQ
5  - A15
6  - A14
7  - A13
8  - A12
9  - A11
11 - A10

Outputs:

12 - SND/PAL8   - to K054321 pin 24
13 - NC
14 - /SBANK_WR  - bank/NMI latch clock (D7, 74LS174)
15 - /SLATCHES  - K054321
16 - /FM        - YM2151
17 - /PCM       - K054539
18 - /SRAM      - MB8464
19 - /SROM      - program ROM

Equations:

/SROM     = /RD & RFSH & /MREQ & /A15 &  A14 +
            /RD & RFSH & /MREQ & /A14                                   ; 0000-BFFF
/SRAM     = RFSH & /MREQ &  A15 &  A14 & /A13                           ; C000-DFFF
/PCM      = RFSH & /MREQ &  A15 &  A14 &  A13 & /A12 & /A11             ; E000-E7FF
/FM       = RFSH & /MREQ &  A15 &  A14 &  A13 & /A12 &  A11             ; E800-EFFF
/SLATCHES = RFSH & /MREQ &  A15 &  A14 &  A13 &  A12 & /A11             ; F000-F7FF
/SBANK_WR = RFSH & /MREQ &  A15 &  A14 &  A13 &  A12 &  A11 & /A10      ; F800-FBFF
/pin13    = RFSH & /MREQ & /A15 &  A14 +
            RFSH & /MREQ & /A14
SND/PAL8  = A15 & A14 & A13 & /A12 & A11 & A10 +                        ; EC00-EFFF
            /M1 & /A15 & A14 +
            /M1 & /A14

/PCM, /FM and /SLATCHES have no A10 term, so each window is 2 kB.
/SBANK_WR has no /WR term: the latch clocks on any F800-FBFF access.
